# Deduplication as flagging preserves judgment calls

A tool that auto-merges suspected duplicates loses nuance: many hits are related-but-distinct ideas sharing vocabulary (e.g., 'decompose-by-tool-fitness' in architecture vs. LLM design). Instead, flag candidates for human review. The tool surfaces the signal; you decide. Flagging also forces explicit awareness of what might be redundant, preventing silent creep.
